Duration Leakage Investigation (Experiment A vs Experiment B)

Critical ML Lesson
What is Target Leakage with the 'duration' Feature? The duration column records the conversation length of the marketing phone call in seconds. If a call lasts 30 minutes, the customer almost certainly agreed to open a deposit. However, at the moment when sales reps prioritize leads before dialing, call duration is unknown (0 seconds). Including duration gives models an unrealistic cheat code during training that is completely useless in real-world pre-call lead scoring.
